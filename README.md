# Beyond the Miles
What shapes a student's sense of belonging at IIT Guwahati?

A quantitative survey study (n = 70) by a team of three, built for Business Statistics and Data Analysis, MBA, IIT Guwahati. It placed 3rd in the Data Tank competition.

## Method
- Survey of students, measuring belonging with the 8-item PSSM scale (Cronbach's alpha = 0.91)
- Analysis in Excel: descriptive statistics, correlation, one-way ANOVA

## Key findings
- Language comfort (r = 0.46) and cultural comfort (r = 0.43) were the strongest drivers of belonging
- Distance from home and home-visit frequency showed no significant effect
